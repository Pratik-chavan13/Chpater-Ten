Why We Named It Chapter Ten

In any great book, the early chapters build the story — but by Chapter Ten, the characters find their true calling, passion peaks, and magic takes over.

That is exactly what happened to our founding team. After years of dreaming, experimenting with sourdough fermentation, and laminating butter pastry dough at 4:00 AM, we decided to turn the page and write our own sweet chapter: a bakery where time slows down, ingredients are unapologetically authentic, and celebration cakes become cherished memories.

Today, every croissant, fudgy brownie, and custom wedding tier leaving our kitchen carries that exact heartfelt devotion.

What Drives Us

Our Four Guiding Baking Pillars

Pure French Butter

Never palm oil or hydrogenated fats. We exclusively fold rich, slow-churned cultured butter for unmistakable flake and mouthfeel.

Dawn-Fresh Every Day

Ovens roar to life at 4:30 AM every morning. What you enjoy was pulled hot from the deck today, never yesterday.

Eggless Mastery

We spent years perfecting velvety sponge formulas so our pure vegetarian customers never compromise on decadent moisture.

Honest Sourcing

From Mahabaleshwar handpicked berries to real Belgian Callebaut couverture chocolate, quality starts in the earth.

Our Journey

Milestones of Chapter Ten

A humble beginning that grew into Pune's favourite boutique celebration bakery.

2021

The Secret Kitchen

Baking small batches of sourdough and fudge brownies for family and close neighbors in Pune. Word of mouth spreads rapidly.

2022

The Perfect Croissant

Months of R&D yield our signature 72-layer honeycomb lamination and 36-hour slow cold fermentation sourdough.

2023

Boutique Studio on MG Road

We open the doors to our brick-and-mortar storefront: Shop No. 5, Rose Garden Complex. A welcoming haven scented with warm vanilla.

2024

Custom Cake Studio Launch

Introducing dedicated bespoke celebration cakes, vintage Lambeth piping frills, and personalized Korean bento boxes with chilled Pune delivery.

2026

12,000+ Celebrations & Beyond

Loved by thousands of pastry lovers across Maharashtra. Continuing our pledge to craft every single bake with pure heart and soul.
