Quick Answers

Event FAQs

How far in advance should we book?

For wedding dessert tables and grand gatherings, we recommend 2 to 4 weeks advance notice. For corporate platters and intimate birthdays, 4 to 7 days is usually sufficient.

Do you provide 100% Eggless event catering?

Yes! Over 80% of our event catering in Pune is prepared completely eggless, including our layered dessert cups, French macarons, brownies, and wedding cake tiers.

Do you handle on-site setup and styling in Pune?

Yes, our pastry styling team can deliver, set up display stands, cake plinths, risers, and floral accents directly at your venue anywhere in Pune.
